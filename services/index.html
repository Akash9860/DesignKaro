Why DesignKaro?

What is DesignKaro?

DesignKaro is a property-services technology ecosystem focused on connecting homeowners, professionals and vendors through a more streamlined digital experience.

Who is DesignKaro for?

The platform is being built for homeowners, skilled professionals, vendors and the teams that coordinate property-service activity.

Where does DesignKaro operate?

DesignKaro is being built for the Indian property-services market. Availability of particular services can vary by location and product rollout.
